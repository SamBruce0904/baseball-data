Analysis: The Two-Way Technological Surge

Tracing Major League Baseball from 2005 (bottom left) to 2025 (top right) reveals an unmistakable diagonal migration: the game simultaneously produced significantly higher strikeout rates and higher exit velocities. Rather than giving an edge to only pitchers or only hitters, tracking technology elevated both disciplines at once. Pitchers leveraged spin tracking and release metrics to drive league average strikeouts from 6.55 K/9 in 2005 to 8.61 K/9 in 2025 (a 31.5% increase). Concurrently, hitters used launch angle and exit velocity analytics to optimize swing paths for hard contact, lifting average hard-hit rates from 30.2% up to 42.1%. This trajectory confirms that technology did not simply tilt the scale toward one side—it redefined the physical standards of the sport.
